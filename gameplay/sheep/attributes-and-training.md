# Attributes & Training

## <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ic_lamb (1) (3).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Sheep Attributes**

Your sheep aren’t just adorable—they’re built for racing. Each sheep comes with unique attributes that directly affect their performance on the track. These attributes are what separate the champions from the rest, making them crucial to your success in the competitive world of [sheep racing](../sheep-racing/).



The rarity of your sheep—whether **Normal**, <mark style="color:blue;">**Rare**</mark>, or <mark style="color:red;">**Epic**</mark>—determines the range of attributes they can unlock. The rarer the sheep, the higher the potential for better attributes. Check out the table below to see the minimum and maximum attribute values for each rarity:

| Rarity | MIN | MAX |
| ------ | --- | --- |
| Normal | 200 | 240 |
| Rare   | 240 | 280 |
| Epic   | 280 | 320 |

### &#x20;<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_dashtime (4).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Dash-Time**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Dash.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

During every sheep race, each woolly contender has their moment of glory—**Dash-Time**. This is when a sheep hits its peak speed, and it’s represented by a **Dashometer** bar alongside their other attributes. The Dashometer gives you real-time insight into when your sheep will surge ahead, allowing you to strategically plan their performance.

## <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_training (5).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Sheep Training&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ill_shepherd_brown-sheep.png (1).PNG" alt="" width="333"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Every sheep in Meta-Land dreams of racing glory, but not all are equally gifted. To unlock their full potential, you’ll need to train them, turning your sheep into true champions. Guiding you on this journey is the mysterious **Shepherd**, a cloaked figure who seems to have descended from the heavens. With a hood that conceals his true identity, this guardian watches over aspiring shepherds, offering wisdom as you shape your sheep's future.



#### Initiating Training

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Training.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click the <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_training (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Sheep Training** button to start boosting your sheep’s abilities. Training helps enhance their attributes, with rarer sheep naturally starting with better traits. The training screen provides all the key details—name, rarity, location, and level—plus easy navigation with left and right arrows to scroll through your flock.

#### Attribute Diagram & Mystery Cubes

On the right side of the screen, you’ll find the **attribute diagram**, a visual map of your sheep’s stats. Every sheep is born with unique attributes, but using [**Mystery Cubes**](../consumable-items.md), you can mix things up. Mystery Cubes give your sheep random stat boosts before you lock in its level up, so you never know what new strengths might emerge.

#### Racing & Rewards

Your sheep aren’t just racing for fun—they’re earning rewards too! Each time they’re placed in the [Racing Barn](../racing-barn.md) and compete, their performance determines the currency they bring in. Train well, race hard, and watch your rewards stack up!



### **Managing Energy**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/energy.png" alt="" width="257"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Each sheep has an **energy bar** that depletes with every race in the Arena. To keep them in peak racing condition, you’ll need to recharge their energy. Use <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_item_EXP_sp_011 (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [**Recharge Potions**](../consumable-items.md) to refill their energy bar and get them back on the track, ready for the next race!



### <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_LvUp (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Levelling Up Sheep

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (45).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To enhance your sheep’s abilities, you’ve got two main options: infuse them with the wool you’ve collected or use <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/601 (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [**EXP Potions**](../consumable-items.md). These potions come in three different sizes, each providing a different amount of experience points. Remember, rarer sheep wool offers more experience, making it a valuable resource for leveling up.



For convenience, there’s an **Auto** button that makes the process simple by automatically calculating and adding the required EXP potions or wool to level up your sheep. Each sheep has a **current stat level** and a **maximum stat level**, represented visually with a _dark brown_ area for the max level and an _orange_ area for the current level. Keep in mind, no stat can be increased beyond its maximum—so if your sheep’s max Balance stat is 170, leveling up won’t ever push it higher than that.



### **Guide: How to Level Up Your Sheep**

1. **Access Sheep Training**: Enter <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_GoFarm (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Sheep Farm mode** and select the sheep you want to level up. Look for the **Sheep Care** panel and click the  <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_training (6).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Training** button to access the training options.
2. **Level Up**: In the training interface, locate the <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_LvUp (3).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Level Up** button on the bottom right. Click to proceed.
3. **Manual or Auto Selection**: A list of required items for leveling up will appear. You can manually choose from your inventory or click the **Auto** button to automatically fill in the necessary components.
4. **Confirm Selection**: After selecting or using the auto-fill option, confirm to begin the leveling up process.
5. **Initiate Leveling Up**: Once confirmed, the leveling up process starts.
6. **Reroll Stats (Optional)**: Once leveling up has started, you can use <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/502 (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Mystery Cubes** as many times as you want to reroll your sheep’s stat boost, before you finalize the level up.
7. **Lock in Level**: After you’re satisfied with the rerolled stats, click **Confirm** to lock in the new level and finalize the process.
8. **Congratulations**: Your sheep is now stronger, with locked-in stats, and ready for bigger races!



### <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_dashtime (5).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Setting Dash-Time**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (17) (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Using the Dashometer

The Dashometer lets you set exactly when, during the race, your sheep will begin their [Dash-Time](attributes-and-training.md#dash-time). You can choose the optimal percentage of the race where your sheep will unleash its speed. Timing it just right can be the key to outpacing the competition.

#### Managing Stamina

While your sheep runs, its stamina gradually depletes, but during Dash-Time, the drain on stamina accelerates dramatically. Pushing your sheep to their limits will give them a significant speed boost, but if their stamina runs out too quickly, they may not finish strong.



Mastering Dash-Time, by balancing speed with stamina, can be the difference between taking the lead and being left behind!



### <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_trash.png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Selling and Resetting Sheep**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2024-10-15 13 15 16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

When your flock grows so large that space is tight—or if one of your sheep is, well, not exactly living up to its potential—you can give them a new home elsewhere by hitting the **Sell** button. Just remember, this decision is permanent, so be ready to say goodbye to your fluffy companion. In exchange, you’ll get back the **EXP** you invested, neatly packaged as <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/601 (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [**EXP potions**](../consumable-items.md).



If you’re not quite ready to part ways but need a do-over, sprinkle some <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Amnesia Dust NEW 250x160 (1) (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [<mark style="color:blue;">**Amnesia dust**</mark> ](../consumable-items.md)on your sheep. This lets them forget their past experiences and start over at level one, and any EXP you've invested in them will be refunded to you as <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/601 (3).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [<mark style="color:blue;">**EXP potions**</mark>.](../consumable-items.md) It’s a great way to rework your sheep’s training without waving goodbye to them!
