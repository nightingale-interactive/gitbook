# Combination Effects

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_3615.PNG" alt=""><figcaption><p>Active Combo Effect Icon</p></figcaption></figure>

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/icon_info (3).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Note**: Combo effects are currently only available for **NFT sheep** on the <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/download.png" alt="" data-size="line"> [Kaia network](https://www.kaia.io/).



Sheep are naturally social animals, and they thrive in the company of their fellow woolly friends. Separating a sheep from its flock can lead to agitation, but grouping them together keeps them happy—and unlocks powerful combo effects!



In sheep farming, **combo effects** can make all the difference. The combinations of sheep you raise can significantly impact the pace of wool production. It’s not just about raising rare sheep; it’s about finding the right combinations. For example, five _**normal**_ sheep with an activated combo effect can often outperform five _<mark style="color:blue;">**rare**</mark>_ sheep without one. The possibilities are endless—explore different pairings and watch your farm thrive!



## <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/icon_check.png" alt="" data-size="original"> **Activation Rules**

Here’s everything you need to know to activate these effects:

* **One Combo Per Pasture**: Each combo can only be activated once within a single pasture. Example:&#x20;
  * Plain White 1 + Plain White 2 = **Ordinary Couple **<mark style="color:green;">**(Activated)**</mark>
  * Plain White 3 + Plain White 4 = **Ordinary Couple **<mark style="color:red;">**(Cannot be activated again)**</mark>
* **Upgradeable Combos**: Some combos can be activated at higher levels with more sheep of the same type. Example:
  * &#x20;Pink Baa-jama Lamb + Pink Baa-jama Lamb = **Counting Sheep (Lv.1)**
  * Pink Baa-jama Lamb + Pink Baa-jama Lamb + Pink Baa-jama Lamb = **Counting Sheep (Lv.2) **<mark style="color:blue;">**(Upgradeable)**</mark>



## <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_3615 (1).PNG" alt="" data-size="line">Available Combo Effects

Mastering the social dynamics of your sheep is the secret sauce to unlocking powerful combo effects that can take your farm to the next level. By grouping the right sheep together, you’ll see your farm blossom with boosted productivity and happier sheep. Don’t be afraid to mix and match—test out different combinations, observe how they interact, and find the ultimate combos to optimize your flock’s performance.
