# Item Shop



<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (25) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>A one-stop shop for all your shepherding needs</p></figcaption></figure>

The local shop offers a diverse range of goods, from unique items and rare treasures to essential supplies for sheep farming and more. It's managed by the friendly and dedicated shop owner, Kelly. After the loss of their parents, Kelly took on the responsibility of raising Katie, and together they’ve built a thriving shop that supports the community.



## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Icon_Item Shop.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Kelly's Item Shop

In the cozy atmosphere of Kelly’s Item Shop, you’ll find a warm welcome and a well-stocked inventory. Items can be purchased using <img src="../.gitbook/assets/item_RB_1 (1) (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [<mark style="color:blue;">Rainbow Beanz</mark>](currency.md) or <img src="../.gitbook/assets/beanz_06.png" alt="" data-size="line"> [<mark style="color:blue;">Seasonal Beanz</mark>](currency.md), with Katie enthusiastically handling all sheep-related transactions in town.



## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Seasonal Shop Tab Icon 150x147.png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Seasonal Shop & Seasonal Decor**

The Seasonal Shop offers a variety of limited-time décor and practical products, available for purchase with <img src="../.gitbook/assets/beanz_06 (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [<mark style="color:blue;">Seasonal Beanz</mark>](currency.md). The shop’s stock changes with the seasons, ensuring fresh and unique items are always on offer. Be sure to visit during the sales period to grab these exclusive items while they last!



## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Bundle Shop Icon 190x190.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Bundle Shop

Kelly also provides bundled item packs at reasonable prices. The Bundle Shop’s offerings are updated regularly, featuring essential combos that are perfect for your farm. Take advantage of these deals while they’re available!



## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (68).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Pop-Up Shop**

The Pop-Up Shop is a temporary section of the store, offering items at a significant discount for a limited time. Stock is limited, and purchases are capped, so don’t miss out on the chance to explore the Pop-Up Shop before it disappears!
