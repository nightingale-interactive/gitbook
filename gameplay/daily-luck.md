# Daily Luck

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2024-10-16 15 36 16.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Daily Luck with Lucky Lenny</p></figcaption></figure>

**Daily Luck** is a fun daily login reward feature hosted by the eccentric Chief of Mallowbu Island, **Lucky Lenny**. Known for embracing life’s unpredictability, Lenny invites you to take a chance each day with a spin on the prize wheel.



## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Lucky coin (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> How It Works

Every day, you’ll get one free spin on the prize wheel, offering you the chance to win exciting rewards. Feel like testing your luck again on the same day? You can use  <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Lucky coin (1) (3).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [<mark style="color:blue;">Lucky Coins</mark>](consumable-items.md) for an extra spin. These coins are available in limited stock from the <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Icon_MARD Shop (8).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **MARD Shop** at a variable price, so grab them while you can!



### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/icon_replay2.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Resetting the Game Board

Once you’ve claimed all the rewards or collected all three available sheep, you can reset the game board and start fresh, unlocking new rewards. The best part? The prizes refresh every season, bringing new items and exciting opportunities to keep you engaged throughout the year.



Embrace the seasonal changes and see what new surprises **Daily Luck** has in store!
