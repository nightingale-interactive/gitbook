# A Shepherd's Beginnings

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/A Shep Beginnings NEW (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**A Shepherd's Beginnings** is your go-to feature for getting a solid start on your journey as a shepherd, all while making the most of your first 100 days in the game. Welcome to the beginning of your sheep-raising adventure!



## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/ic.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Getting Started

The game kicks off with a quick tutorial led by Michelle, introducing you to **A Shepherd’s Beginnings** and the essentials of shepherding. After the tutorial, you can jump into the feature at your own pace. Every day brings a new lesson in caring for your flock, helping you grow as a shepherd.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

From day one, you’ll learn the basics, and as you progress, daily missions will continue to build your skills—taking you from beginner tasks to preparing your sheep for the excitement of races.

#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/main_UI3_bt_favorite (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Daily Missions

* **Day 1**: You’ll start with the fundamentals—grooming, feeding, and caring for your sheep.
* **Day 2 and Beyond**: Each new day unlocks a mission, getting a bit more challenging as you go.

#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/ic_racingTime00.png" alt="" data-size="line">  Progress at Your Own Pace

* **No Rush**: While missions unlock daily, you’re free to complete them whenever you’re ready within the first 100 days.
* **Catch-Up Friendly**: Miss a day? No worries! You can always go back and complete previous missions at your convenience.

#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/star (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Rewards

Each mission comes with rewards that are vital for your progress—items, currency, and tools that will help you succeed as a shepherd.

#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/master_badge (1).PNG" alt="" data-size="line"> Your Journey Awaits

Over the next 100 days, **A Shepherd’s Beginnings** will guide you through all the must-know basics of shepherding and sheep racing. By the end, you’ll have everything you need to tackle more advanced challenges and grow your flock like a pro!
