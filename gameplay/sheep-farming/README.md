# Sheep Farming

## <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/icon_GoFarm (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Getting Started

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_3462 (1).jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Begin your exciting journey to becoming a skilled shepherd alongside Michelle and her faithful companion Meryl. With the wisdom of a mysterious legendary shepherd guiding you, you’ll transform into the shepherd every sheep dreams of. Raising racing champions isn’t easy, but the rewards make the journey worth every step. As you build your flock, you’ll create a close-knit family in the enchanting world of Meta-land.



## <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/178b2d1d592c433adadae31a40f81a20.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Game Access

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (28) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The game is available across multiple platforms, including _**iOS, Android, and Web Browsers**_, allowing you to easily import and export your account between them. When you start your adventure, you’ll be given a starting wallet address (account). But a word of caution—if you forget to export this account code, it may be permanently lost when importing another account to your device.



This flexibility lets you seamlessly transfer your game progress and all your hard-earned assets from one device to another, so you can always pick up right where you left off.



## <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/main_ui2_bt_decos (1) (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Starter Pack

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2024-02-05 17 32 03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Starting a new account? Lucky you—you’ll receive a valuable starter pack to kick off your adventure. This includes Meryl, Michelle’s loyal companion, as your first sheep. You’ll also get a pasture and some decorative items to give Meryl the perfect home. As you take on missions and challenges, you’ll unlock more items and sheep, adding to the fun and expanding your farm.
