# Real-world & Meta-land

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (52).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Visit our [<mark style="color:blue;">website</mark>](https://sheepfarm.io/) and explore the Inventory menu, which is divided into two categories: Real World and Meta-Land. The Real World category represents your crypto wallet address, while Meta-Land is the in-game environment.



To access certain features and activities, you'll need specific items in either the Real World or Meta-Land. You can easily switch between the Real World and Meta-Land tabs, sort items by category, search by name or ID, and identify the network (if it's an NFT) through badges in the corner of each item. Items not associated with NFTs will be marked as _**Non-NFT**_ and cannot be moved to the Real World.





## **Guide: Moving NFT Items to Meta-Land**

1. **Wallet Connection**: While on the game's [website](https://sheepfarm.io/), make sure your wallet is connected by clicking on the wallet button in the top right corner.
2. **Real World Inventory**: Navigate to the inventory menu on the website. Look for the tab labeled "Real World" and click on it to access your inventory of real-world items.
3. **Select Item**: Within the Real World inventory, locate the item you wish to transfer to the in-game environment, Meta-Land. Only NFT items eligible for transfer.
4. **Confirm Transaction**: After selecting the item for transfer, follow any additional on-screen instructions to complete the transaction. This may involve confirming the transfer and providing authorization from your connected wallet.
5. **Complete Transfer**: Once you've confirmed the transaction, the item will be moved to Meta-Land within the game. The transfer process may take a moment to complete, depending on network conditions.
6. **Transfer Completion**: After the item is successfully moved to Meta-Land, you can enjoy using it within the game. Explore Meta-Land and interact with the item as you would with any other in-game asset.

_<mark style="color:red;">Note:</mark>_

* _<mark style="color:red;">Transferring items of this nature entails a blockchain transaction, which may incur an associated fee.</mark>_



## **Guide: Moving NFT Items to Real-World**

1. **Wallet Connection**: While on the game's [website](https://sheepfarm.io/), make sure your wallet is connected by clicking on the wallet button in the top right corner.
2. **Meta-land Inventory**: Navigate to the inventory menu on the website and click on the "Meta-land" tab. This tab is where you can manage items that you want to transfer from the in-game environment to the real world. _Game items in use can't be transferred to the real world, like a racing or grazing sheep NFT. Place them in your in-game inventory before attempting transfer._
3. **Select Item**: Within the Meta-land inventory, locate the NFT item you want to transfer to the real world.
4. **Confirm Transaction**: After selecting the item for transfer, follow any additional on-screen instructions to complete the transaction. This may involve confirming the transfer and providing authorization from your connected wallet.
5. **Complete Transfer**: Once you've confirmed the transaction, the NFT item will be moved from Meta-Land to the real world. The transfer process may take a moment to complete, depending on network conditions.
6. **Transfer Completion**: After the item is successfully moved to the real world, it will no longer be visible or accessible in the in-game environment (Meta-Land). It is now a tangible asset that you can possess outside of the game.

_<mark style="color:red;">Notes:</mark>_

* _<mark style="color:red;">Transferring items of this nature entails a blockchain transaction, which may incur an associated fee.</mark>_
* _<mark style="color:red;">If you attempt to transfer an NFT item to the real world, you might encounter a message that reads "Items used in game cannot be transferred to the real world". To resolve this, remove the item from any activity (such as an NFT sheep racing or grazing on a pasture) so it's in your in-game inventory before you attempt the transfer.</mark>_
