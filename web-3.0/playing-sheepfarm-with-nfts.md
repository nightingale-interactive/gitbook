# Playing Sheepfarm with NFTs

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/money2_02.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Playing with NFTs in the game opens up a world of possibilities. You can acquire NFTs like pastures, sheep, and decorations from platforms like [**Opensea** ](https://opensea.io/collection/sheepfarm)to enhance your in-game experience. These virtual assets can be seamlessly transferred between Meta-land and the real world via our [**website**](https://sheepfarm.io/).



#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/ic_racingList.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Accessing Your Collection

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2024-01-22 16 19 58 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Viewing your **SheepFarm NFTs** is simple—just connect your wallet to the [inventory ](https://sheepfarm.io/inventory)menu on the website. Any item that’s an NFT will have a small badge indicating its network, making it easy to spot which of your in-game treasures are NFTs.



#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/icon_LvUp.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Benefits of Playing with NFTs

NFTs bring real benefits to your farm. You can [**sell the wool**](playing-sheepfarm-with-nfts.md#selling-nft-sheep-wool) produced by your **NFT sheep** for <img src="../.gitbook/assets/ic_mard_big2 (2) (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [**MARD tokens**](../gameplay/currency.md#mard-tokens-the-currency-of-opulence), giving you a steady income. Plus, if you ever want to transfer your NFTs to another wallet or sell them on third-party marketplaces, you have complete ownership and flexibility over your items.



#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Icon_Minting Voucher EPIC (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Minting Non-NFT Items

In an exciting new twist, you can now [mint your non-NFT in-game items into NFTs](playing-sheepfarm-with-nfts/minting-nfts.md), offering even more potential for building your collection. It’s a great feature for NFT collectors looking to expand and enhance their virtual assets.

## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/gold6__50p.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Selling NFT Sheep Wool

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2024-01-22 15 57 56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can sell the wool produced by your _**NFT sheep**_ to **Marge**, a regular visitor to sheep farms who’s always on the hunt for high-quality wool. The rarity of your sheep determines the wool’s value—rarer sheep produce more valuable wool. Marge is a well-known figure in **Meta-land**, famous for her fashion expertise. She turns locally-sourced wool into stylish clothing and is deeply committed to supporting local shepherds, helping to enrich the sheep farming culture in the village.



### **Guide: Selling the Wool of NFT Sheep**

1. **Access Inventory**: While in Sheep Farming mode, navigate to the right-hand side of your screen to access your <img src="../.gitbook/assets/icon_stuff (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> inventory.
2. **Locate Wool Tab**: Inside your inventory, click on the <img src="../.gitbook/assets/img_wool_6100 (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Wool** tab.
3. **Initiate Selling Process**: If you have wool from NFT sheep, a <img src="../.gitbook/assets/icon_sell (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Sell** button will appear next to it. Click it to begin selling your wool to Marge.
4. **Select Quantity**: Choose the amount of wool you want to sell and confirm your choice.
5. **Receive MARD Tokens**: Once confirmed, the MARD tokens from the sale will be added to your in-game balance.

## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/ic_search (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Reveal Your NFT Sheep’s Attributes**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Every sheep has its own distinct personality and hidden [attributes](../gameplay/sheep/attributes-and-training.md), just waiting to be uncovered. Strengthen your connection with your sheep by revealing their unique characteristics and unlocking their potential.



### Guide: Revealing NFT Sheep's Stats

1. **Link Your Wallet**: Make sure your wallet is connected on the game's website. Click the wallet button in the top right corner and follow the prompts to securely connect your wallet.
2. **Visit Sheep Reveal Tab**: Head over to the **Reveal** tab in the **Racing** menu on the website. Click to proceed.
3. **Select Sheep**: On the **Sheep Reveal** page, click the **Select Sheep** button and choose the NFT sheep you want to reveal. You’ll be prompted to select from your collection.
4. **Confirm Reveal**: Confirm the reveal process, which costs **50** <img src="../.gitbook/assets/item_RB_1 (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Rainbow Beanz**. Ensure you have enough Rainbow Beanz before continuing.
5. **View Detailed Stats**: Once the process is complete, you’ll be able to see your sheep’s detailed racing attributes and stats.
6. **Manage Revealed Sheep**: You can now view all sheep whose stats have been revealed. Easily navigate through the list, filter by **My Reveal**, or search using a token ID number to view specific sheep.

## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/sheepfarm-video-promo COPY_EXTENSION 1.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Morphing NFT Sheep

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Morphing Page</p></figcaption></figure>

Sheep Morphing is an exciting feature exclusively for <img src="../.gitbook/assets/download (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> [**Kaia Network**](https://www.kaia.io/) NFTs. If you're looking to give your flock an upgrade, morphing is your ticket. By pairing two sheep of the same rarity, you’ll have the chance to unlock a sheep of even higher rarity. While success isn’t guaranteed, the best part is that your original sheep won't lose any value.



In Meta-land, some sheep are so rare they can _**only**_ be unlocked through morphing! You never know what kind of unique sheep you might end up with!



#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/icon_info (3) (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> How It Works

To begin morphing, you’ll need **one male** and **one female** sheep of the same rarity. Just a heads-up: two sheep of the same gender won’t work. Once you start the process, there’s no turning back, but with a bit of luck, you might end up with something truly special:

* **Normal (male) + Normal (female)** = Normal / Rare / Epic Sheep
* **Rare (male) + Rare (female)** = Rare / Epic Sheep



#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/icon_stuff (2) (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> What You’ll Need

* **Matching Sheep**: Two sheep of the same rarity in your wallet, and they must be male and female.
* **MARD Tokens**: Make sure you’ve got enough MARD tokens to cover the morphing fee.



Morphing can be a bit of a gamble, but it's a great way to add something new and rare to your flock!

For more details, check out the full summary here: [Morphing Decor Upgrade Summary](https://www.notion.so/ngit-interactive/MORPHING-DECOR-UPGRADE-SUMMARY-587602db8d014636b7ba7700160f87fc).



### **Guide: Morph Your NFT Sheep**

1. **Access the NFT Menu**: Start by heading over to the website’s **NFT** menu.
2. **Choose Sheep Morphing**: Inside the NFT menu, click on the **Sheep Morphing** tab to get started.
3. **Select Your Sheep**: Pick one male and one female sheep of the same rarity from your collection. These are the sheep you’ll morph.
4. **Initiate Morphing**: Once your sheep are selected, begin the morphing process. There will be a  <img src="../.gitbook/assets/ic_mard_big2.png" alt="" data-size="line"> standard fee to proceed.
5. **Find Your New Sheep**: After the morphing is complete, your newly morphed sheep will appear in the **Real-world** section of the **Inventory** menu.



<img src="../.gitbook/assets/symbol.png" alt="" data-size="line"> _<mark style="color:green;">**Important Factors to Consider**</mark>_

1. <mark style="color:green;">**No Lower Rarity**</mark><mark style="color:green;">: Morphing will</mark> <mark style="color:green;"></mark><mark style="color:green;">**never**</mark> <mark style="color:green;"></mark><mark style="color:green;">result in a sheep of lower rarity than the ones you started with. Your newly morphed sheep will either maintain the same rarity or rise to a higher one.</mark>
2. <mark style="color:green;">**Original Sheep Exclusion**</mark><mark style="color:green;">: The original sheep you select for morphing won’t be part of the outcome. That means you won’t get the same sheep you started with.</mark>
3. <mark style="color:green;">**Irreversible Process**</mark><mark style="color:green;">: Once you start the morphing process, there’s no going back. This transformation is permanent, so make sure you’re certain before proceeding.</mark>
4. <mark style="color:green;">**Patience is Key**</mark><mark style="color:green;">: Morphing takes time! Depending on server stability, the process may take a bit, so be patient as your new sheep is being created.</mark>

## <img src="../.gitbook/assets/item_popup_up_tab__50p-hd.png" alt="" data-size="line"> **NFT Decor Items and Upgrades**



**NFT Decorative items** can either be used as-is or _upgraded_ to give your wool production a serious boost. These upgrades are done through the **NFT menu** on the website, using other decor items as resources. Just keep in mind—this feature is exclusive to <img src="../.gitbook/assets/download (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> **Kaia NFTs**, and there’s no guarantee the upgrade will always succeed.



#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/icon_level.png" alt="" data-size="original"> Upgrade Levels

* **Level 0**: Where it all begins. Minimal effect on wool production.
* **Levels +1 to +5**: Each level bumps up productivity, making your decor items more impactful with each upgrade.
* **Combo Effects**: No matter the item level, active combo effects always work just the same.



#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/middle_view_purple-star.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Effects

How much your wool production speeds up depends on the **level** and **surface area** of the decor. Items with the same surface area have similar effects, but as your decor gets bigger and stronger, the impact increases—along with the materials needed to level them up!



#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/icon_weather (2).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Weather-Related Properties

Remember, every decor item has weather-related properties that can really boost its efficiency. Keep an eye on the weather for some extra benefits!



#### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/icon_add2 (3).png" alt="" data-size="line"> Effects Calculation

Decor and combo effects are **cumulative**—they add up, instead of multiplying. This means smart combinations are the key to squeezing the most out of your wool production.



For the full breakdown, check out the comprehensive summary [here](https://www.notion.so/ngit-interactive/MORPHING-DECOR-UPGRADE-SUMMARY-587602db8d014636b7ba7700160f87fc).



### **Guide: Upgrade an NFT Decor Item**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (1) (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. **Access Decor Upgrade Tab**: Head to the [**Decor Upgrade**](https://sheepfarm.io/upgrades) tab in the **NFT menu** on the website.
2. **Select Decor Item**: Pick the decor item you want to upgrade from the list.
3. **Choose Upgrade Materials**: Select several decor items to use as materials for the upgrade. The more items you add, the higher your chances of success. Keep in mind, these items will be permanently lost during the process.
4. **Confirm Upgrade**: Confirm the upgrade, and your decor item will be enhanced! The upgrade requires <img src="../.gitbook/assets/ic_mard_big2 (1) (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> 1 [**MARD token**](../gameplay/currency.md#mard-tokens-the-currency-of-opulence) to complete.
