# Removed Networks & Minting

<details>

<summary>Oasys Minting (Removed)</summary>

**How to Mint NFTs on** [**Oasys - HOME Verse network**](https://www.oasys.games/)

1. **Selecting an item for minting**:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Visit the [website](https://sheepfarm.io/inventory), find your in-game item, and choose to mint it into an NFT.



2. **Confirm the purchase**:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (11).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Double-check the minting price and pay using **OAS tokens**.



3. **Sign your wallet**:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (12).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Confirm the transaction by signing your wallet to activate the smart contract.



4. **Completion**:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (13).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Once the process is finished, your shiny new NFT will appear on the website.



5. **NFT in inventory:**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled (14).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* You can transfer, trade, or use your NFT back in the game [**\[Meta-land\]**](../web-3.0/playing-sheepfarm-with-nfts/real-world-and-meta-land.md) for all your in-game adventures.



Minting NFTs opens up new opportunities for your in-game assets, giving you the freedom to trade, collect, and enhance your virtual collection!

### Kaia Network Notes (Removed)

### Old Marketplaces (Removed)



Now you can take your in-game assets to the next level by minting **non-NFT** items into **NFTs**. This exciting feature is available exclusively on the [**Oasys** ](https://www.oasys.games/)and network, allowing you to transform standard in-game items into NFTs. While minting does require a small fee or specific [consumables](../gameplay/consumable-items.md), it's a straightforward way to unlock extra value and opportunities with your virtual assets.
